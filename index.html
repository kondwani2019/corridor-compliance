1. Document Verification
Invoice Details Commercial invoice fields · USD
Invoice References & Dates

Invoice number, purchase order, order date, invoice date, due date, and payment terms link the goods to the commercial transaction and establish its audit chronology.

Seller / Exporter

Identifies the party issuing the invoice. The legal name, address, and registration details help customs and auditors match the invoice to the exporter.

Buyer / Importer

Identifies the importer and delivery party. Matching these details to declarations and transport documents helps prevent ownership, consignee, and destination discrepancies.

Product Details

List each product separately with description, HS code, origin, quantity, unit, and unit price. These details support classification, valuation, origin, and line-by-line document reconciliation.

Shipping & Sale Terms

Incoterms and the named place identify delivery responsibilities and help explain which freight, insurance, and risk costs are included in the declared value.

Price Build-up (USD)

Separating freight, insurance, duties, other charges, and discounts makes the declared invoice total traceable to its components.

Fields marked * and all applicable customs requirements must be completed. Invoice requirements vary by jurisdiction; confirm with the relevant customs authority or broker.
